
SHOPEASE - WARRANTY POLICY

1. Warranty Period
Eligible electronic products come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date.

2. Warranty Coverage
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal usage.

3. Warranty Exclusions
Physical damage, liquid damage, unauthorized repairs, and accidental damage are not covered.

4. Warranty Claim
Customers must provide their purchase invoice and product serial number to initiate a warranty claim.

5. Inspection
The technical team will inspect the product before approving a warranty claim.

6. Replacement and Repair
Depending on the inspection result, the product may be repaired or replaced.
