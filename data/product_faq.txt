
SHOPEASE - CUSTOMER FAQ

1. How can I track my order?
Customers can track their orders using their order ID through the order tracking service.

2. How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

3. Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

4. How can I contact customer support?
Customers can contact support through the customer support portal.

5. What payment methods are supported?
Customers can pay using UPI, debit cards, credit cards, and net banking.

6. How can I check product availability?
Customers can check availability on the product details page.
