
SHOPEASE - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

1. Return Eligibility
Customers can return eligible products within 10 days of delivery.

2. Product Condition
Products must be unused and returned with their original packaging, accessories, and invoice.

3. Non-Returnable Products
Personalized products, perishable items, and products marked as non-returnable cannot be returned.

4. Refund Processing
After the returned product passes inspection, refunds are initiated within 5 to 7 business days.

5. Refund Method
Refunds are processed through the original payment method.

6. Damaged Products
Customers who receive damaged products should report the issue within 48 hours of delivery and provide photographs.

7. Return Request
Customers can contact customer support to initiate a return request.
